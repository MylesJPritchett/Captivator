# Captivator

Captivator lets businesses visually design the branded page shown to people when they connect to the business's WiFi.

## People

**Customer**:
The business using Captivator to design its Splash Page.
_Avoid_: User, client, owner

**Guest**:
A person connecting to the Customer's WiFi who sees the Splash Page. Never uses the Editor.
_Avoid_: Visitor, end user, user

## The design

**Splash Page**:
The branded page a Guest sees when joining the WiFi; the thing a Customer designs. Each has a name the Customer chooses; only the Customer sees it, and Reset keeps it.
_Avoid_: Captive portal, landing page, template

**Library**:
The Customer's saved Splash Pages in this browser. Never empty.
_Avoid_: Gallery, collection, saved pages, projects

**Open Splash Page**:
The one Splash Page from the Library that the Editor and Preview are showing.
_Avoid_: Current page, active page, selected page

**Starter Splash Page**:
The ready-made Splash Page the Editor opens on, and what Reset returns to.
_Avoid_: Default, template, blank page

**Logo**:
A single image the Customer uploads to brand the Splash Page.
_Avoid_: Image, brand image

**Background**:
What sits behind the Splash Page's content: either an uploaded image or a solid colour.
_Avoid_: Backdrop, theme

**Text Block**:
A piece of Customer-written text on the Splash Page with its own colour and size. A Splash Page can have several.
_Avoid_: Label, heading, paragraph, text element

**Input Field**:
A field the Guest fills in before connecting, of type Name, Email or Phone. A Splash Page has at most two, and never two of the same type.
_Avoid_: Form field, input, question

**Connect Button**:
The button a Guest presses to join the WiFi; the Customer sets its background and text colours.
_Avoid_: Submit button, CTA

## The tool

**Editor**:
The panel where a Customer changes the Splash Page.
_Avoid_: Builder, designer, settings

**Preview**:
A live rendering of the Splash Page as a Guest would see it on the chosen Device.
_Avoid_: Canvas, mockup, render

**Device**:
The screen size the Preview simulates: Mobile or Desktop. The Customer toggles between them; the Preview shows one at a time.
_Avoid_: Viewport, breakpoint, mode
